Entry 1: Deciding on My Topic (Law)
Choosing a Topic
The topic I chose for my year-long project is law. I have wanted to become a lawyer since the fourth grade, and my interest in law has continued to grow. I enjoy watching documentaries about real cases, reading about them, and learning how they are solved. I also like debating, solving mysteries, and playing mystery games in my free time.
Another reason I am interested in law is that my dad used to be a police officer, which inspired me to learn more about the legal system. I enjoy reading about different laws, how they were created, and how they affect people's lives. I also know that lawyers can make good money, which is another reason I see this as a possible career for my future. I chose this topic because law is something I am passionate about and can imagine myself doing for a living.
Engineering Design Process (EDP)
I am currently in the first stages of the engineering design process, where I am defining my topic and researching it. For my project, I want to learn more about what lawyers do, the different types of law, and what it takes to become a lawyer.
I plan to research different legal cases, learn how laws are created, and explore how lawyers prepare for court. I also want to understand the education and training requiredto enter this career. This research will help me learn more about the legal field and decide which area of law interests me the most.
Skills
Some skills I hope to develop through this project are research, critical thinking, communication, and time management. These skills are important for lawyers because they need to understand cases, find information, and explain their arguments clearly.
Research Skills
Research is important in law because lawyers need to understand laws, examine evidence, and learn about past cases. For my project, I will practice finding reliable information and taking notes about different legal topics. Since I already enjoy reading about cases and watching documentaries, I think this will be a good opportunity to improve my research skills.
Critical Thinking
Critical thinking is important because lawyers need to look at situations from different perspectives and use evidence to support their arguments. I enjoy solving mysteries and playing games that challenge me to think. Through this project, I want to learn how lawyers analyze cases and figure out the best way to approach different situations.
Communication
Communication is another important skill because lawyers need to explain their ideas and defend their arguments. Since I enjoy debating, I think this is a skill I can build on. I want to improve how I express my opinions, listen to different perspectives, and support my arguments with facts.
Time Management
Time management is important because I need to balance my project with my other responsibilities. I plan to set goals and make time for research so I can stay organized and finish my work on time. This will help me become more responsible and manage my time better.
Next Steps
Moving forward, I plan to research different types of law, especially criminal law, because I am interested in cases and investigations. I also want to learn how laws are created, how court cases work, and how lawyers prepare their arguments. Another goal is to understand the steps needed to become a lawyer, including the education and training required. I hope this research helps me learn more about my future career.
Summary
Overall, I chose law for my Freedom Project because I have wanted to become a lawyer since the fourth grade. My interest in real cases, documentaries, debating, and solving mysteries has made me even more interested in this career. My dad's experience as a police officer has also inspired me to learn more about the legal system. Through this project, I hope to understand how laws work, how lawyers solve cases, and what I need to do to become a lawyer. I also want to improve my research, critical-thinking, communication, and time-management skills.
