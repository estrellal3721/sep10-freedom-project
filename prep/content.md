## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
##### Westlaw
  * Helps lawyers research law and court cases 
  
  * Provides accsess to legal documents 
  
  * Helps lawyers prepare arguments for court
  
  * Used by lawyers, judges, and law students.
  
  * Works on Windows and Mac computers.

  *Website: https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/products/westlaw
  
 ##### Clio
* Helps lawyers organize their cases.

* Keeps track of appointments and deadlines.

* Stores important client information.

* Helps manage payments and legal documents.

* Works on computers, iPhones, and Android devices.

* Website: https://www.clio.com

 ##### DocuSign
* Allows people to sign legal documents online.
  
* Helps lawyers send contracts to clients.
  
* Saves time by avoiding paper documents.
  
* Keeps electronic signatures organized.
  
* Works on computers, iPhones, and Android devices.
  
* Website: https://www.docusign.com
  
  ##### Adobe Acrobat
* Allows lawyers to read and edit PDF documents.

* Helps create important legal paperwork.

* Allows users to highlight important information.

* Lets clients sign documents electronically.

* Works on Windows, Mac, iPhones, and Android devices.

* Website: https://www.adobe.com/acrobat.html



##### Hardware
##### Digital Evidence Forensic Workstation
* Helps lawyers and investigators examine evidence from computers and phones.

* Can recover deleted files, messages, and digital information.

* Helps find evidence for criminal cases.

* Used in investigations involving cybercrime.

  ##### Electronic Courtroom Evidence Presentation System
* Allows lawyers to display evidence on courtroom screens.

* Can show photographs, videos, documents, and recordings.

* Helps judges and juries understand complicated evidence.

* Makes courtroom presentations easier.
 ##### 3D Crime Scene Scanner
* Uses laser technology to create a 3D model of a crime scene.

* Records the exact positions of objects and evidence.

* Helps lawyers explain what happened at a crime scene.

* Can be used in criminal investigations.
##### Portable Biometric Fingerprint Scanner
* Scans fingerprints to help identify individuals.

* Can be used during criminal investigations.

* Helps verify someone's identity.

* Provides digital fingerprint records
